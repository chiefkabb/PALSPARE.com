GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might wonder.

What is PalSpare?+

PalSpare is a group savings platform in development. The aim is to help eligible members organise contributions and rotating payouts through a clear digital experience.

How do group payouts work?+

In a rotating group, members contribute on an agreed schedule. The pooled amount is paid to one member per round according to agreed rules. Members continue contributing after receiving their payout.

Can anyone join a savings group?+

No. PalSpare plans to use identity, affordability and other eligibility checks before people can join. Availability will depend on the platform's launch and applicable regulatory arrangements.

Is PalSpare available to use today?+

Not yet. The product shown is a development preview. You can register your interest in future updates; this is not an application to join a live financial product.
